About Niagara Custom Water

Niagara Custom Water is a customized bottled drinking water branding and supply business based in Bangladesh.

Our main goal is to help businesses create their own identity through personalized drinking water bottles.

We believe that branding is not limited to signboards, menus, packaging, or advertisements. Even a simple bottle of drinking water can become a powerful representation of a business.

That's why we provide customized water bottle solutions where businesses can showcase their own logo, brand name, colors, and personalized label designs.

We aim to make customized bottled water accessible to restaurants, hotels, cafés, offices, corporate organizations, and event organizers.

At Niagara Custom Water, we believe every bottle has the potential to represent a brand.
